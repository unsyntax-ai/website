Building a Two-Sided Security Marketplace

CredNation (Now Doberme)

December 2024
6 months
3 engineers

The Challenge

The founders needed a complete tech foundation built from scratch with two different user types requiring different workflows. The platform had to be investor-ready, stable yet scalable for long-term growth.

  • No existing tech foundation - everything built from scratch
  • Two different user types (guards and clients) with distinct workflows
  • Needed investor-ready MVP quickly
  • Had to be stable yet scalable for future growth
  • Any delay risked investor confidence and market entry timing

Our Solution

We designed and engineered a full two-sided marketplace MVP with modular architecture for rapid iteration without technical debt.

MVP Development

  • Guard onboarding, verification & licensing workflows
  • Job posting and real-time matching system
  • Guard availability and scheduling logic
  • Client dashboards and job management tools
  • Secure authentication and role-based access
  • Clean, reliable backend built for speed and expansion

Rapid Launch & Funding

  • Delivered stable, investor-ready MVP in 4 weeks
  • Enabled early user onboarding and confident pitching
  • Founder secured funding at $12M valuation within one month of launch

Production Scale-Up

  • Expanded platform from MVP to full production system
  • Implemented new UI/UX from design team
  • Enhanced match logic and job flow
  • Improved stability, speed, and reliability
  • Added features for both guard and client operations

The Impact

  • Clear product-market validation with significant early valuation
  • Stable, scalable V2 handling complex two-sided workflows
  • Professional-grade UX suitable for enterprise clients
  • Automated workflows improving operational efficiency
  • Strong technical foundation supporting multi-state expansion
